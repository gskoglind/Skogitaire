How to Play

  • Build the four foundations up by suit, Ace to King — hearts, spades, diamonds, clubs, left to right.
  • In the columns, stack cards down in alternating colours. Only a King starts an empty column.
  • Drag cards, or double-click one to place it automatically.
  • Click the deck to deal three at a time. When it runs out, click again to recycle.
  • Switch to Draw 1 any time — useful for finishing a deal that has stalled.
  • If you sit still a moment, a card will pulse to show a move you missed.
  • Once the deck is empty and every card is face up, Auto-Finish appears and plays out the rest.
